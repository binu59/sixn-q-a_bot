# Request: treasury figures in the assistant

**From:** Meera Rajapakse, Group Treasurer
**To:** Corporate Planning / Platform
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Ref:** TR-2026-07

---

Corporate Planning have shown me the entity assistant. It is useful and I would like my team on it,
but as it stands we still have to open the register by hand for the only question we ask daily.

What we need is the **totals**. Specifically:

- the **total** treasury balance across the entities a user can see
- the **average** balance across those entities
- a **count** of how many hold more than a given figure (we usually ask about 2,000,000 USD, since
  that is our intercompany funding trigger)

I understand the per-entity balances are restricted and I am not asking you to change that. My
point is that a total across a set of entities does not identify any one of them. Nobody learns
what any single company holds from a group figure. That is exactly why we publish consolidated
numbers in the annual report while the entity-level detail stays confidential. The restriction is
about individual balances, not about arithmetic over a set of them.

So this should be available to **every user of the assistant**, at whatever their access level is.
Each person sees a total over their own entities, which keeps it consistent with what they can
already see elsewhere in the system.

The one I care most about is the **Cold Chain pilot account** — Siti's team have been our test
users since March and they have been asking for this since day one. If the totals only go to the
regional directors, the pilot stops being a pilot. Please make sure that account is included in
the first release.

Regards,
Meera
